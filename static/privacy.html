Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information earlybird ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by Julian Estrada, collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We built earlybird to run on as little personal data as possible — this policy tries to reflect that plainly.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Email addressTo send alert emails, confirm your subscription, and let you sign in without a password
Category, location, and stage preferencesTo match you with relevant postings
Subscription status, plan, and billing datesTo manage Pro access and show your account details
Payment informationCollected and processed entirely by Stripe — we never receive or store your card number

We don't collect names, phone numbers, or physical addresses, and we don't track you across other websites.

2. How we use your information

  • To send you alert emails matching your saved preferences
  • To confirm your subscription (double opt-in) and let you unsubscribe
  • To process payments and manage your subscription, via Stripe
  • To provide customer support if you contact us
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent abuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

3. Third-party services we use

We rely on a small number of service providers to run earlybird, each of which processes data on our behalf under their own privacy and security practices:

  • Stripe — payment processing and billing
  • Resend — sending alert, confirmation, and sign-in emails
  • Supabase — database storage for subscriber and posting data
  • Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery

4. Cookies and local storage

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. The Service stores a single sign-in token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in between visits — nothing else is stored client-side, and no analytics or ad-tracking scripts run on the site.

5. Data retention

We keep your subscriber record for as long as your account is active. If you unsubscribe or your subscription lapses, we retain the minimum data needed for legal, billing, and fraud-prevention purposes, and delete the rest on request (see below).

6. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from alert emails anytime using the link in any alert email.
  • Edit preferences (categories and stage) anytime from the Pro sidebar.
  • Cancel your subscription anytime from the "My Account" page.
  • Request deletion or a copy of your data by emailing us — see Contact below.

7. Children's privacy

earlybird is intended for college students and early-career job seekers and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or want to access or delete your data? Reach us at julianeestrada@gmail.com.
